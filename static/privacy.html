Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

BXK Trader Pro respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, protected, and disclosed in connection with the BXK Trader Pro service.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, contact information, mobile telephone numbers, application preferences, system activity, and information necessary to provide trading-monitoring and account-related services.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate and secure BXK Trader Pro, authenticate authorized users, provide requested application features, monitor system performance, deliver account-related notifications, and provide optional transactional SMS alerts.

SMS Messaging and Mobile Information

Mobile Messaging Privacy: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data, consent records, and mobile telephone numbers will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be provided only to service providers solely as necessary to operate and deliver the requested SMS messaging service.

Users who expressly opt in to BXK Trader Pro SMS alerts may receive transactional messages concerning monitored trading positions, overnight risk conditions, risk-state changes, recovery notifications, and other operational account information.

Message frequency varies based on monitored conditions and account activity. Message and data rates may apply. SMS participation is optional. Users may opt out of SMS messaging at any time by replying STOP or by disabling SMS alerts for their account.

Service Providers

BXK Trader Pro may use third-party service providers solely as necessary to operate the service, including hosting, communications, security, and related technical services. These providers receive information only as necessary to perform services on behalf of BXK Trader Pro. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, satisfy legitimate business and security needs, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices

Users may decline optional SMS notifications or discontinue them at any time. Disabling SMS notifications does not prevent use of other available BXK Trader Pro features.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The current version will be published at this URL with its most recent revision date.
