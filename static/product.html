Core platform features

The application combines market context with trade-building and risk-management tools.

Market Intelligence

SPX and supported-underlying analysis, expected move, volatility context, market scoring, and live option-chain inputs.

Trade Construction

Defined-risk strategies including iron condors, credit spreads, debit spreads, butterflies, and configurable probability targets.

Position Monitoring

Open-position monitoring, risk-state analysis, profit and loss context, threatened-side detection, and configurable alerts.

Performance Tools

Trade journaling and performance-oriented workflows designed to help users review how their trading process is behaving over time.

SMS Alerts

Optional transactional text alerts for monitored trading-risk conditions. SMS enrollment requires explicit user consent.

Subscription Access

Account-based access with monthly and annual billing options through Stripe-hosted checkout and customer billing management.

Broker connectivity

BXK Trader Pro uses a broker-abstraction architecture so supported users can connect their own brokerage accounts. Broker authorization is user-specific and is intended to use broker-provided authentication and authorization flows.

Current production connectivity includes Tastytrade integration. Schwab Trader API connectivity is being prepared for commercial review and is not represented as generally available until the applicable commercial onboarding and approvals are complete.

Account and security controls

Per-user access

Accounts, broker connections, trading permissions, subscriptions, and optional alerts are associated with the authenticated user.

Protected credentials

Broker-connection credentials are handled through application-side security controls rather than being exposed in the browser UI.

User control

Users remain responsible for their brokerage accounts, orders, positions, and risk, including reviewing order details before submission.

Important trading disclosure

BXK Trader Pro is a decision-support and trading-workflow platform. Trading options involves substantial risk and is not suitable for every investor. Platform calculations, market data, alerts, and trade structures may be delayed, unavailable, or affected by third-party services. Users are responsible for evaluating trades and monitoring their own brokerage accounts.

BXK Trader Pro does not guarantee investment results or that any market condition, trade condition, or risk event will be detected.
