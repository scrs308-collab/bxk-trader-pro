Getting Started

Sign in to Trader Pro

Open app.bxktraderpro.com to reach the secure BXK Trader Pro login screen.

  1. Enter your BXK username.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Select SIGN IN.
  4. Complete a password change if the application requires one.
Use Create New Account to request access. Use Forgot Password? if you cannot access your account.

Chapter 3

Connect Your Broker

Broker connections allow BXK Trader Pro to retrieve supported account and position information.

Tastytrade

  1. Open the broker connection controls.
  2. Select Connect Tastytrade when available.
  3. Sign in directly at Tastytrade.
  4. Authorize BXK Trader Pro.
  5. Select the brokerage account BXK should use if more than one account is returned.
Your Tastytrade password stays with Tastytrade. BXK uses broker authorization rather than storing the broker password.

Account Data Source vs. Execution Broker

BXK separates the broker used to read account data from the broker permitted to submit orders. The application displays these responsibilities separately so the source of each action remains clear.

Tastytrade is the current execution path for supported live orders. Schwab account-data connectivity will be available when the applicable integration is enabled.

Build a Trade

The Trade Parameters section controls the setup BXK evaluates.

Control Purpose
Underlying Select SPX or another supported symbol.
LOAD Loads option discovery data.
Strategy BXK Auto or a supported strategy.
Expiration Select the desired DTE.
Wing Width Select spread width.
Contracts Select requested position quantity.
  1. Confirm the underlying.
  2. Select the strategy.
  3. Choose expiration and wing width.
  4. Choose contract quantity.
  5. Select BUILD TRADE.
Some non-SPX underlyings may display OBSERVATION ONLY. BXK may analyze these setups while blocking execution.

Chapter 6

Evaluate the Trade Candidate

When BXK finds a qualifying candidate, Mission Control displays the complete proposed trade.

Mission Status ENTER TRADE or stand aside.
Mission Score Trade-quality score.
Confidence BXK confidence level.
Execution READY or BLOCKED.
Credit Proposed net credit.
POP Probability of profit.
Max Risk Maximum defined trade risk.
Buying Power Estimated buying-power requirement.
If the setup is not approved, BXK displays NO TRADE or Stand Aside.

For an approved setup, select ENTER TRADE to open the order-review workflow.

07
Chapter 7

Review the Order

Selecting ENTER TRADE opens the BXK Trade Review window. It does not immediately submit the order.

If the review banner says BXK LIVE MODE, successful submission can place a real broker order.

Final Risk Check

  1. Order preview created.
  2. Positive credit.
  3. Maximum risk defined.
  4. Buying power calculated.
  5. All required option legs present.
  6. Execution status ready.

Verify expiration, DTE, limit credit, probability of profit, maximum profit, maximum risk, buying power, quantity, and every option leg.

BXK checks passing does not replace your own order review. Confirm that the order shown is exactly the order you intend to place before submitting it.

Chapter 8

Monitor the Position

Open Position Monitor to review supported positions returned by your selected brokerage account.

Open P/L Current position profit or loss.
Profit Captured Progress relative to maximum profit.
Position Structure Option legs and position type.
Trade Values Opening and current position values.

BXK Position Coach

Position Coach provides strategy-aware guidance such as HOLD, CONSIDER EXIT, CLOSE POSITION, REVIEW NOW, or EXPIRATION DAY.

If BXK displays a quote-quality warning, live valuation may be unreliable and automated P/L guidance may be suspended.

Chapter 9

Market Summary & Strategies

Market Summary

Market Summary displays the broader market context feeding the BXK decision engine, including SPX, volatility, expected move, market score, stability information, and risk history.

Strategies

The BXK Strategy Playbook compares supported strategy structures and displays the current assessment, score, confidence, and risk profile for each.

Chapter 10

Review Performance

Open the Performance tab to review trade-journal statistics and recent completed trades.

The dashboard can display completed trade count, win rate, realized P/L, average trade, profit factor, average winner and loser, risk escalations, and recent trade history.

Select REFRESH to reload the current journal statistics.

Owner-level administration provides supported controls for managing Trader Pro users and access.

Use Trader Pro on Mobile

BXK Trader Pro uses the same responsive application on desktop and mobile.

  1. Open app.bxktraderpro.com in your mobile browser.
  2. Sign in using your normal BXK account.
  3. Navigate through Trade Setup, Position Monitor, Market Summary, Strategies, and Performance.
  4. Scroll through cards vertically as the desktop layout adapts to the narrower screen.
