Privacy Policy

Draft for product development. Have final commercial privacy terms reviewed before accepting paid subscribers.

Information we may process

BXK Trader Pro may process account registration information, application preferences, broker authorization data, brokerage account metadata, positions, balances, trade-related records, device or session information, and support communications as needed to provide the service.

Broker credentials

Where broker OAuth is supported, BXK Trader Pro is designed to store encrypted authorization tokens rather than ask users to disclose broker passwords to BXK. Third-party brokers remain responsible for their own authentication and brokerage services.

How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate users, connect authorized brokerage accounts, provide product features, maintain security, diagnose problems, and improve the application.

Third parties

BXK Trader Pro may rely on infrastructure, communications, analytics, and brokerage API providers. Their handling of data is governed by their own agreements and privacy practices where applicable.

Retention and security

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service and to satisfy applicable legal or operational requirements. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Updates

This policy may be revised as the product, broker integrations, and commercial offering develop.
