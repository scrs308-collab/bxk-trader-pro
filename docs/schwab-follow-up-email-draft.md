For the current commercial review, Schwab is treated as an account-data integration. BXK does not represent Schwab order execution as generally available unless and until the approved commercial product, scopes, and applicable agreements permit it.

The application includes per-user broker authorization, account isolation, encrypted token storage, masked account information, owner-controlled Broker Connect permissions, user-controlled disconnect, and separate live-trading permissions.

Preliminary user estimates are approximately 10-25 users during the initial controlled rollout and approximately 50-100 users during the first year, subject to adoption and onboarding pace.
